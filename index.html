How to play

  • Upgrade production buildings (Lumbermill/Clay/Iron/Farm).
  • Houses increase population cap. Barracks/Archer Camp/Stable enable training.
  • Town Center speeds construction. Wall reduces mission casualties. Market lowers trade fee.
  • Use Town Plaza to send missions; rewards scale with difficulty and Plaza level.
