How to play

  • Upgrade Lumbermill / Clay Mine / Iron Mine / Farm to generate resources over time.
  • Upgrade Houses to raise Population Cap. Units consume population.
  • Barracks / Archer Camp / Stable unlock and speed training of Infantry, Archers, Cavalry.
  • Town Center reduces construction time. Wall reduces mission casualties. Market reduces trade fee.
  • Use the Town Plaza to send troops on missions (waves of monsters). Rewards scale with difficulty and Town Plaza level.
  • Game autosaves. Use Wipe Save to start over.
